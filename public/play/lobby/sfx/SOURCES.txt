Sounds of the waiting room (S3). All are cuts of recordings from the Sonniss #GameAudioGDC bundles
(royalty-free, commercial use allowed), downloaded from the official your.org mirror into vr-arena/_sound-src
and cut with lobby/tools/sfx/sfx.py (trim, fade, mono, level). Nothing synthesized.

Used for: vote = vote confirmed; hover = laser moves onto a game; tick = countdown 3-2-1 and setup point touched;
go = countdown end and the vote winner; bell = operator message and end message; results = scores shown;
click = small confirmations (swap hands, height set).
