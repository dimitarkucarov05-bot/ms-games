# The owner's feedback after playing The Motel 2.3.0 with two players on real headsets

2026-11-13, translated from Bulgarian, meaning kept.

- "There were extremely many bugs in the game we played as two. This was the worst game I have played."
- Sounds: "You have to download a base library of sounds. You cannot tell me your sounds are some beeps. You do not
  feel that you are shooting; the controllers should vibrate when you shoot. The whole game experience must be felt."
- "The graphics you got more or less OK."
- Boss: "A bit dumb, I killed the boss by holding fire at his head for 5 seconds. He should jump somewhere, walk
  somewhere, be interesting."
- Zombies: "They walked almost all the same, there were none that jump. Find a zombie that shoots a bow, one that
  throws something radioactive; when you kill it the place becomes radioactive for some 10 seconds. I do not want to
  give you the ideas: I want YOU to invent a whole working concept for this game and finish it from start to end.
  It has a lot of potential."
- Weapons: "The chainsaw does not feel like a chainsaw. The axe I was holding by the tip at its end; it does not feel
  like holding an axe."
- The real column in his room, drawn as an obstacle in the zone: "You must isolate it, it has to be some object,
  because now I walk into it like a complete idiot."
- Gates: "I cannot reach the outer things to repair them. It must be a bit tighter so I reach them. And for the
  repairing the hammer should simply lie on the ground."
- Hands: "You should hold things with the inner buttons (the two grip buttons): with them you grab objects, and they
  fall from your hands when you let go. Easy to drop, easy to grab hammers and repair. That is the right direction."
- Voice: "Find some free AI tool that makes action voices. 'Right behind you!' 'Good to know!' A more interesting,
  dynamic, deep voice, so it feels like a game. With you it does not feel like a game."
- Chest: "I did not even understand that the chest had opened. That dumb. No sounds, no effects."
- Fear and dynamics: "It was not scary at all. There must be dynamics: they should jump out at you more, come closer,
  jump over things. If they have to come by helicopter they fall from above, by plane with parachutes: you figure it
  out."
- Motel sign: "The motel sign was glitching into something. Check how the textures on the motel are arranged."
- Darkness: "At some moment it can become hyper dark and zombies come and you do not expect from where; almost
  blindness, you light only with the flashlight; all lamps go out with a cool effect; on the next wave they come back.
  For example a truck cuts all the power."
- Assets: "Use only textures that already exist in the libraries. You have full access to all our libraries. Be
  creative about what in them could be useful."
- Skins: "After we go from the lobby to the game there must be a mid lobby in the game's theme, for example in that
  tent: we enter the tent and there are different skins as small figures, you pick one and you become it. Add a secret
  skin, a bear for example, or the strangest thing you can find."
- "I forbid AI-slop objects and sounds. If it is not downloaded and not made properly, do not praise it to me.
  We rely only on textures and ready asset packs."
- "Next time I ask to play, I want to enjoy it to the max."

## Facts measured during that session

- About 45 frames a second in the game (90 in the lobby) on a Quest 2, even with no zombies on screen: 216 draw
  calls, about 300k triangles per eye, 6 lights, full-resolution buffer (2880 x 1584).
- His test room is small: an L-shaped zone of about 10 square metres (two rooms 2.4 x 2.3 m and 1.6 x 3.1 m) with one
  column of about 0.55 x 0.35 m inside it. Real arenas are up to about 11 x 11 m. The game must work in both.
- What the game has now (2.3.0): pistol, smg, revolver, machete, shotgun, axe, carbine, rifle, hunting rifle,
  chainsaw, lmg; a shared ammo chest that opens between waves and throws weapons on the ground; 5 waves + a boss;
  gates of six planks that zombies break and players nail back; difficulty levels; titles as bold text; all sounds
  synthesized (rejected); zombie animation clips available: idle, walk, crouch walk, run, sprint, stumble, four
  attacks, hit reactions, stagger, four deaths, two roars, menace, rise.
- The Synty animation library on the LAN server also has (not used yet): jumps (idle, running, sprinting), leaping
  attack, in-air fall, hard/medium/soft landings, knock-down begin/loop/end, stun, dodge rolls, a whole bow combat set
  (draw, aim, shoot, leaping shot, deaths by hit place), goblin locomotion (hunched gaits), emotes (roars, chest
  beating, taunts, dances).
- Models seen in the catalogue: 50 city zombies (among them a biohazard-suit zombie, a hot-dog-suit zombie, a military
  zombie, a diver, kids), 4 boss zombies, crossbow and bows with arrows, parachutes, military helicopters (also
  crashed), chemical barrels, a radioactive sign, generators, power poles with lines, a transformer, flashlights,
  military and quarantine tents, mannequins, about 1,400 hero characters in 170 packs.
